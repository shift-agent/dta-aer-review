Catherine & Alex’s Park Crest Wedding

Real Wedding Park Crest Event Facility · Birmingham, AL

Catherine and Alex’s Park Crest wedding was absolutely stunning — the weather was perfect, the colors were perfect, and the details were perfect. How much more perfect can it get? Catherine chose deep fall colors, a soft touch of drapery, garland and dupioni linens, and our favorite finishing touch: flameless candles.

When we arrived at Park Crest on this lovely Saturday in November, wedding planner Becky Baker of Becky’s Brides was smiling from ear to ear — practically skipping with excitement over this beautiful day and the adorable couple.

Catherine used subtle colors for her table linens — but not the cake table. Oh, the cake table! How much do you love this burgundy dupioni linen? The soft, creamy drapery provided a beautiful entrance for guests and a stunning backdrop for the couple’s photographs.

And if we haven’t expressed our love for flameless candles lately, please let us apologize. We love these candles. With the right product they look completely realistic, they keep our linens safe, and they keep venues and fire marshals happy. Everyone wins.

A Park Crest wedding is always stunning, but Catherine’s was something special — deep rich fall color, a soft touch of drapery, garland and dupioni linens, and those flameless candles, all working together to make the day pop.
