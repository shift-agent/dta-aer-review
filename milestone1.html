Decisions we made to get there

How the design was shaped — keeping your intent intact.

1

Keeping your look, raising the finish

The design keeps the site’s established character — your language, your palette — while elevating typography, spacing and consistency to a cleaner finish.

2

Modeled on — not copied from — the example website

We studied how the example site works, then built our own versions of the functions that serve you — your site, not a clone.

3

Removed sizes, adopted your structure

Size listings stay in Current RMS. The storefront organizes products by your spreadsheet’s structure — fabric, design, color, in your order — and we’re adopting the photo naming convention alongside it.

4

Bonus: resolved the quote engine challenge

Quotes now write priced line items into Current RMS automatically — proven live, no re-keying.
