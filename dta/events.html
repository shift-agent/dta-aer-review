Common questions

How far ahead should we book?

Most clients reach out three to six months before the date. We'll happily hold a conversation earlier for peak-season weekends.

Do you set up and break down?

Yes — complete setup and breakdown is our standard service, coordinated with your venue and planner.

Can you work with linens we already own?

We do. We also offer washing and pressing for venues and individuals who own their own linens.

Planning something lovely?

Tell us the date, the venue and the palette. We'll build the look with you.
