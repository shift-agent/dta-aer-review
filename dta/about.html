About Decor To Adore

A family-owned event rental company, helping clients bring their vision to life with ease, intention and style.

Our Story

Every celebration has a story

Decor to Adore is a family-owned event rental company led by Barrett and his wife, built on a shared love for design, hospitality, and creating unforgettable celebrations. What started as a passion for beautiful gatherings has grown into a full-service rental company dedicated to helping clients bring their vision to life with ease, intention, and style.

We specialize in providing thoughtfully curated event rentals for weddings, corporate events, and special occasions.

Our Philosophy

Every detail matters

At Decor to Adore, we believe every detail matters. From the texture of a linen to the placement of a light, our goal is to create a cohesive and elevated experience that photographs beautifully and feels effortless in person. We work closely with planners, venues, and clients to ensure every element comes together seamlessly.
