Decisions we made to get there

How the design was shaped — keeping your intent intact.

1

Kept your look, raised the finish

Matched the staging site’s language and palette, then tightened the type, spacing and a squared, elevated UI — same intent, cleaner execution.

2

Two ways in: product & event

Added a “by event” path on top of category browsing, so a client can start from “I’m planning a wedding” — your goal of easy discovery.

3

Solved the quote at the source

Mapped the quote form to real Current RMS line items so it prices automatically — the fix for the hand-off, pending a write-scoped key.

4

Honored “no prices, quote-based”

Kept the storefront free of rates and size rows, exactly as you run it — everything routes to a quote.
