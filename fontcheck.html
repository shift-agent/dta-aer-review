Reading it: if the “Cormorant Garamond” column looks identical to the “Georgia” column, Cormorant is not loading — that’s the bug, separate from the Cormorant-vs-Playfair choice. Playfair Display is a higher-contrast serif (thin/thick strokes, more vertical) that reads closer to a classic logotype; Cormorant is softer and more calligraphic, especially in italic.

Once we pick, the robust fix is to self-host the chosen font so it always renders — no dependency on Google Fonts or a browser’s ad/tracker shields.
