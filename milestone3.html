08 · Milestone 3

Inventory Complete & Site Ready

Milestone 2 locked how the site looks and behaves. This one fills it: the whole catalog correctly categorized and photographed, every page and flow tested end to end, and the two content areas still to be built — the social feed and venue galleries.

Target date

What Milestone 3 covers

Four workstreams. The first two finish what exists; the last two add what is missing.

1

Completed inventory

Every category correctly mapped, confirmed against the client’s own product spreadsheet, and photographed to a consistent standard. Today the catalog holds 963 products / 2,223 options across the twelve home page categories, with 93 options showing real photography.

Three things gate “complete”: the twelve-versus-six category reconciliation (Milestone 2, item 1), the 87 photographs still awaiting identification, and the genuine shooting gap that only becomes visible once those are placed.

2

End-to-end site and content testing

A full pass over every page and every flow with real content in place — not spot checks. Browse by product and by event, product pages, add to quote, the quote cart, the full quote request, the contact form, and every static page.

Two of those do not exist yet: the contact form, and the deliberate connection between it and the quote request so the two read as one intentional path rather than two separate tools. Both are Milestone 3 work.

3

Social area

The Stories / Journal area is live and now styled to match the home page cards, but it is running on imported historical posts. This workstream covers the social plugin proper — how posts are published going forward, whether feeds pull from the client’s own social accounts, and what the team maintains after launch.

Needs a decision on scope before it can be estimated: a maintained on-site journal, a pulled social feed, or both.

4

Venue galleries

A gallery per venue, so a couple can see the room they have booked already dressed. The photography is largely in hand — 22 venues, 142 photographs already sitting in the shared Drive folder.

What is undecided is the shape: whether a venue is a page of its own, how a gallery relates to the inventory used in it, and whether venues become a browse axis alongside product and event.

What has to be settled first

Milestone 3 cannot be scoped or dated until these are answered — each is an open call from Milestone 2.

⚠

The category structure

Twelve categories on the home page, six on the inventory page. Until they agree, “completed inventory” has no fixed target and the home page cards cannot link into the collection.

⚠

The Current RMS key

Still returning 401. The catalog runs from the last good snapshot, which is safe, but nothing syncs and no quote can be written back until a fresh key is issued.

⚠

Photography ownership

Who shoots the remaining gap, and to what standard. The naming convention and the publishing path are proven — 163 photographs went live in one pass today — so this is a question of supply, not of mechanism.
