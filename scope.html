01 · Project ScopeWhere we are

The short version: the risky, uncertain parts are behind us. What's left is a decision about what to launch first and how you'll manage it.

Your data is healthy

The Current RMS ID worry had a simple, harmless cause — a stale local copy. After a refresh, 99.6% matched. Nothing corrupted, nothing to rebuild.

The hard part works

Current RMS lists every size separately. The system already folds those into one browsable product with selectable options.

Both sites are designed

Decor To Adore and Alabama Event Rentals are built as working pages on your real catalog — home, shop, product, quote — with live search across all products.

One real constraint

~85% of the catalog has no sellable photograph. This gates launch far more than any code does — which is exactly why we phase it.

And not every category is shown publicly. 02 · SolutionsWhat we deploy — Phase 1 & Phase 2

Rather than wait for the whole catalog, we launch what's ready now and grow it. Two phases, each with a clear finish line.

Phase 1 · Launch what's ready

A real site, in front of customers

Decor To Adore live on the collection that's already photographed — not held behind the photography backlog.

  • Publish the ready categories — Flatware, Glassware, Dupioni, Chairs, Lighting and the linen ranges.
  • Browse-first storefront + quote requests into Current RMS.
  • Live Current RMS connection, product grouping, brand tagging.
  • Managed in Simplitory inside WordPress — no code.
  • Back-office groups (Cleaning, Fees, Labor) excluded from the storefront.

Outcome: a browsable, on-brand site your customers can use — proving the whole pipeline end to end.

Phase 2 · Extend & scale

Full catalog, second brand, automation

Repeat the working pipeline across everything, and take the manual steps out.

  • Extend product structuring to the full catalog.
  • Add Alabama Event Rentals from the shared inventory.
  • Automatic refresh — the permanent fix for the ID issue.
  • Itemised, priced quote → opportunity, no re-keying.
  • Photography filled category by category, live as it lands.
  • SimpleSuite enhancements layered in (step 5).

Outcome: both brands, the complete catalog, and a site that keeps itself current.

Phase 3 · Grow — after launch. With the site live and the catalog complete, the SimpleSuite tools turn it into a growth engine — marketing analysis and decisions (Pulse), search & answer-engine visibility (SEO & AEO), and event bookings. Not part of the launch scope; the conversation for once you're live and confident.
On dates. We're not committing to a timeline before scope is agreed — that's what stalled this before. Agree Phase 1 today and we return a phased plan with dates we'll hold ourselves to, plus a regular demo of working progress.
